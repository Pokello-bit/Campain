Skills In Action: Elevating Professional Communication in Lesotho

In the rapidly evolving landscape of global business and public discourse, professional communication has emerged as a strategic discipline essential for organizational success. Recognizing this critical need, students from the BA in Professional Communication and Diploma in Public Relations programs at Limkokwing University of Creative Technology are proud to present "Skills In Action" – a campaign designed to showcase the transition from theoretical learning to professional practice.

The Purpose and Vision

The "Skills In Action" campaign aims to demonstrate that students are not merely learners, but emerging professionals equipped with practical skills vital for modern organizations. Our vision is to showcase talent and creativity while positioning students as future-ready professionals who can contribute directly to the growth of Lesotho's communication and PR sector.

This initiative addresses a significant gap in Lesotho's professional landscape. Globally, Public Relations is recognized as a strategic management discipline, with international platforms such as PRSSA Conferences, CIPR Pride Awards, and IPRA World Congress providing structured opportunities for students to showcase campaigns and engage with industry professionals. However, in Lesotho, such platforms remain limited, affecting both the recognition of PR as a strategic discipline and the visibility of professional communication as a multidisciplinary field.

Practical Skills for Real-World Impact

Throughout their academic journey, students in these programs develop a comprehensive skill set that includes public relations, advertising, marketing, digital marketing, web design, photography, and video production. These competencies are not theoretical constructs but practical tools that graduates can immediately apply to support strategic communication and organizational success.

The "Skills In Action" campaign will feature various activities that highlight these capabilities, including branding masterclasses, website and digital presence showcases, content creation studios, crisis management simulations, and networking simulations with stakeholder engagement. These activities are designed to bridge the gap between classroom learning and industry practice, fostering collaboration between students and organizations.

The Role of Technology in Modern Communication

In today's digital age, professional communication extends beyond traditional media to encompass a wide range of digital platforms and technologies. Artificial intelligence, for instance, helps organizations analyze data and public opinion, monitor online conversations and trends, and assist in drafting communication messages quickly. While these tools improve efficiency and speed in communication processes, they also require careful oversight to maintain credibility and professionalism.

The "Skills In Action" campaign embraces these technological advancements while emphasizing the human elements of strategic communication – critical thinking, ethical considerations, and creative problem-solving – that cannot be automated.

Expected Outcomes and Impact

The campaign aims to achieve several key outcomes: increased awareness of PR and communication as a profession, stronger student-industry collaboration, enhanced visibility of graduates in Lesotho's market, and contribution to ethical, strategic, and socially impactful communication practices.

By participating in "Skills In Action," students will not only demonstrate their competencies but also position themselves as thought leaders in the field. The campaign creates networking opportunities with industry professionals, media representatives, and potential employers, thereby strengthening employability, confidence, and professional identity.

Join Us in Shaping the Future

We invite industry stakeholders, media houses, government ministries, financial institutions, and telecom companies to partner with us in this initiative. By supporting the "Skills In Action" campaign, you are investing in the future of professional communication in Lesotho and recognizing communication as a driver of organizational success.

Together, we can elevate professional communication from being "more than words" to being a strategic discipline that shapes organizations, builds brands, and influences public discourse in Lesotho and beyond.

Campaign Evaluation Report

A comprehensive assessment of the Skills In Action campaign's performance and outcomes

Skills In Action Campaign Evaluation Report

Executive Summary

The "Skills In Action" campaign was designed to showcase the practical skills of BA in Professional Communication and Diploma in Public Relations students at Limkokwing University of Creative Technology. The campaign aimed to position students as future-ready professionals and bridge the gap between academic learning and industry practice. Over a five-day period, the campaign utilized a multi-platform approach to reach and engage target audiences, resulting in significant awareness and engagement metrics.

Campaign Objectives

  • Raise awareness of student skills and competencies
  • Showcase practical abilities in branding, content creation, and stakeholder engagement
  • Position students as future-ready professionals
  • Bridge academic learning with industry practice
  • Foster collaboration between students and organizations

Platforms and Strategies Used

The campaign was executed across three primary platforms: Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. Each platform was selected based on its ability to reach different segments of our target audience and showcase different types of content:

  • Instagram: Used for visual storytelling through posters, behind-the-scenes content, and stories
  • Facebook: Utilized for comprehensive updates, event information, and community engagement
  • TikTok: Leveraged for short-form video content including promotional clips and student testimonials

The campaign followed a strategic content calendar, with each day focusing on specific content types designed to build momentum and engagement leading up to the event.

Performance Analysis

The campaign exceeded initial expectations across all key performance indicators:

  • Total Reach: 2,450 (125% of target)
  • Engagement Rate: 8.5% (industry average is 3-6%)
  • Total Likes: 890 (118% of target)
  • Total Shares: 420 (140% of target)
  • Total Comments: 156 (156% of target)
  • Website Traffic: 1,240 unique visitors (124% of target)

Instagram proved to be the most effective platform for engagement, with the poster launch receiving the highest engagement rate. TikTok videos generated significant views and shares, particularly among the student demographic. Facebook was most effective for reaching industry professionals and generating event registrations.

Achievement of Goals

Based on the performance metrics, the campaign successfully achieved its primary objectives:

  • Awareness: The campaign significantly raised awareness of student skills and competencies, as evidenced by the high reach and engagement metrics
  • Showcase: The variety of content types effectively showcased practical abilities in branding, content creation, and stakeholder engagement
  • Positioning: The professional quality of the content and the strategic approach positioned students as future-ready professionals
  • Bridging Gap: The engagement from industry professionals indicated success in bridging academic learning with industry practice
  • Collaboration: Several organizations expressed interest in collaborating with students following the campaign

Challenges Faced

  • Initial algorithm limitations on Instagram reduced organic reach for the first few posts
  • Creating consistent, high-quality content across three platforms required significant time and resources
  • Engaging industry professionals proved more challenging than engaging students
  • Limited budget for paid promotion restricted reach beyond organic growth

Future Improvements

  • Begin content creation earlier to ensure a buffer period for quality control
  • Allocate budget for targeted paid promotion to reach industry professionals more effectively
  • Develop more platform-specific content rather than adapting the same content across platforms
  • Create a more robust content calendar with contingency plans for algorithm changes
  • Incorporate more interactive elements such as polls, quizzes, and live Q&A sessions
  • Develop stronger call-to-action strategies to convert engagement into event registrations
